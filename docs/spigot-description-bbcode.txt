[CENTER]
[B][SIZE=6]YouNeedMe[/SIZE][/B]

[SIZE=4]An all-in-one, Essentials-style administration and utility plugin for [B]Paper, Spigot, Purpur and Folia[/B] servers.[/SIZE]

It combines homes, warps, economy, shops, auction house, moderation, kits, teleportation, integrations and much more in one modern and configurable plugin.

[URL=https://github.com/Mathildeuh/YouNeedMe]GitHub Repository[/URL] · [URL=https://github.com/Mathildeuh/YouNeedMe/wiki]Wiki[/URL] · [URL=https://mathildeuh.github.io/YouNeedMe/]Javadoc[/URL]
[/CENTER]

[B][SIZE=5]Features[/SIZE][/B]

[B][SIZE=4]Economy & Commerce[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Built-in economy with native Vault support
[*][B]/balance[/B], [B]/pay[/B], [B]/baltop[/B] and administration commands
[*]Configurable server shop with categories and an in-game editor
[*]Auction house with real bidding and anti-duplication protections
[*]Item selling and worth commands
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]Homes, Warps & Teleportation[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Multiple homes with permission-based limits
[*]Public and categorized warps with costs and cooldowns
[*]Safe random teleport with [B]/rtp[/B]
[*]Complete teleport request system: [B]/tpa[/B], [B]/tpahere[/B], [B]/tpaccept[/B], [B]/tpdeny[/B], [B]/tpcancel[/B], [B]/tpaignore[/B], [B]/tpaqueue[/B] and [B]/tpatoggle[/B]
[*]Spawn management, back and death-location teleportation
[*]Teleportation to an offline player's last location
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]Moderation & Administration[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Temporary and permanent bans
[*]IP bans, mutes and kicks
[*]Punishment history
[*]Vanish and god mode
[*]Gamemode and flight management
[*][B]/invsee[/B] and [B]/endersee[/B]
[*]Staff dashboard with [B]/ynm panel[/B]
[*]Broadcast, sudo and player management tools
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]Kits & Quality of Life[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Permission-based kits with cooldowns
[*]One-time and limited kit claims
[*]In-game kit management
[*]Nicknames, AFK status and playtime tracking
[*]Personal time and weather
[*]Virtual anvil, crafting table, stonecutter and trash
[*]Item renaming, repairing and enchantment management
[*]Full localization support
[*]Player support tickets
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]Integrations[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*]Vault
[*]LuckPerms
[*]PlaceholderAPI
[*]DiscordSRV and webhooks
[*]Floodgate
[*]TAB
[*]Redis network synchronization
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=4]Storage[/SIZE][/B]

Choose the storage backend that fits your server:

[LIST]
[*]SQLite — enabled by default
[*]MySQL
[*]MariaDB
[*]PostgreSQL
[*]MongoDB
[*]Flat JSON storage
[/LIST]

Switching storage backends only requires changing one configuration option.

[B][SIZE=4]Folia Support[/SIZE][/B]

YouNeedMe is designed with Folia compatibility in mind. Scheduled tasks use a dedicated scheduler abstraction instead of directly relying on Bukkit's scheduler.

The built-in sidebar scoreboard is automatically disabled on Folia because Folia does not currently support Bukkit's scoreboard API. A Folia-compatible scoreboard plugin can be used instead.

[B][SIZE=5]Integrations & Placeholders[/SIZE][/B]

YouNeedMe integrates with popular server plugins and exposes its own PlaceholderAPI expansion.

Available placeholders include:

[LIST]
[*]Balance
[*]Homes
[*]Nickname
[*]AFK status
[*]Vanish status
[*]Playtime
[*]LuckPerms group
[*]Prefixes and suffixes
[/LIST]

[B][SIZE=5]Installation[/SIZE][/B]

[LIST=1]
[*]Download the latest version from [URL=https://github.com/Mathildeuh/YouNeedMe/releases/latest]GitHub Releases[/URL].
[*]Put [B]YouNeedMe.jar[/B] in your server's [B]plugins[/B] folder.
[*]Restart your server.
[*]Configure [B]plugins/YouNeedMe/config.yml[/B].
[*]Customize modules in [B]plugins/YouNeedMe/modules/[/B].
[*]Run [B]/ynm reload[/B].
[/LIST]

No database setup is required to get started. YouNeedMe uses SQLite by default.

[B][SIZE=5]Requirements[/SIZE][/B]

[LIST]
[*]Java 21 or newer
[*]Paper, Spigot, Purpur, Folia or a compatible fork
[*]Minecraft 26.2 or newer
[/LIST]
